### 🧠 About Me

Data Scientist in **GenAI R&D at NICE Actimize**, building production **LLM systems** for **fraud and financial-crime detection** on platforms screening **billions of transactions daily**. I take applied research from prototype to deployed product — designing **agentic RAG** architectures, running rigorous **model evaluation**, fine-tuning open-weight LLMs, and embedding **responsible-AI and model governance** into everything I ship.

- 🔭 **Now:** production Generative AI for regulatory reporting & financial crime — RAG, evaluation (RAGAS / LLM-as-a-judge), guardrails, drift monitoring
- 🧪 **Background:** MS Physics (IISER Pune) → published research on **sparse system identification (SINDy)** for nonlinear dynamical systems
- 🏆 **Highlight:** Top Prize, **MIT Boston Hackathon** — climate–health risk scoring in 48h
- 🌱 **Exploring:** agentic systems, LLM evaluation at scale, physics-informed ML
- 💬 **Ask me about:** RAG, LLM fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), MLOps, or shipping AI in regulated domains
